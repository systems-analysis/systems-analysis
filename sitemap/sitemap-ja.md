## Status of the Japanese static branch

The Japanese static branch is under construction. Only the articles listed above are published. [Sections](https://systems-analysis.ru/ja/sections.html) and [Site map](https://systems-analysis.ru/ja/map.html) present the full planned tree — systems analysis, operations research, decision theory, modeling, systems theory, management and planning, systems engineering, and data science — so some entries there point to pages that are still being added. Do not assume that a slug present in the English or Russian branch already exists under `/ja/`. For a subject that is not yet available in Japanese, use the international-wiki routes below, or the English static branch at [https://systems-analysis.ru/en/](https://systems-analysis.ru/en/).
